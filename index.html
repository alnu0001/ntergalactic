001 THE VISION

Humanity's expansion beyond Earth represents an expansion of scientific knowledge, economic opportunity, technological capability, strategic strength, and the physical reach of civilization.

Space opens access to new environments, resources, scientific observations, industrial possibilities, and markets. The development of a mature space economy can create industries spanning transportation, energy, communications, autonomous systems, advanced manufacturing, resource utilization, computation, research, habitation, and tourism.

Operating in extreme extraterrestrial environments also demands breakthroughs in energy efficiency, recycling, water purification, autonomous robotics, medicine, materials, computation, communications, food production, thermal management, and closed-loop systems. These capabilities can flow back into terrestrial applications and improve productivity, resilience, sustainability, and quality of life on Earth.

Extraterrestrial resources create another frontier. The Moon, asteroids, and eventually other planetary bodies may provide materials and resources that support infrastructure beyond Earth. As extraction, processing, manufacturing, and transportation technologies mature, civilization can progressively build economic systems capable of sourcing an increasing share of their requirements from beyond Earth.

Commercial human spaceflight adds another dimension. As transportation technologies improve and costs decline, space tourism can develop from an exceptional experience into a broader commercial industry, supporting hospitality, transportation, training, entertainment, research, and eventually destinations beyond Earth.

For the United States, leadership in space strengthens scientific capacity, advanced manufacturing, communications, navigation, autonomous systems, critical infrastructure, industrial competitiveness, and national security. Space capabilities already support essential civilian and defense functions, making technological leadership in this domain strategically consequential.

These forces reinforce one another.

Scientific discovery creates technology. Technology creates capability. Capability creates industry. Industry creates resources for further discovery.
And every expansion of capability gives humanity more tools to improve life on Earth and extend civilization farther beyond it.

NTERGALACTIC vision is to accelerate humanity’s scientific and technological advancement toward a civilization capable of operating across planetary systems, reaching neighboring star systems, and ultimately developing physically viable means of transportation across intergalactic distances.

We recognize the scale of that final objective. Under humanity's current experimental physics, massive objects cannot reach or exceed the speed of light, and intergalactic distances make conventional propulsion inadequate on human timescales. NTERGALACTIC therefore recognizes intergalactic transportation as a fundamental scientific and engineering problem: advancing what known physics permits while rigorously investigating whether future discoveries in propulsion, energy, spacetime, and fundamental physics can expand what is physically achievable.
